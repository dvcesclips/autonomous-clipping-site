A clear, controlled process

Clipping that starts with permission.

Autonomous Clipping is designed for people and teams working with video they own or are authorized to use.

  1. 01

    Bring authorized video

    Start with source material you created, own, or have permission to repurpose.

  2. 02

    Prepare short clips

    Identify moments that fit a vertical, short-form format and prepare them for review.

  3. 03

    Review before publishing

    Keep control of your voice, context, and publishing decisions at every step.

  4. 04

    Publish to TikTok

    Use the TikTok integration only for content you choose to publish through your connected account.
